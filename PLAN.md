# CS206 — Design Thinking Workshop — PLAN

**Semester:** 2 · **Credits:** 2 · **Level:** Beginner
**Theory:** 15 hrs · **Lab:** 30 hrs · **Tools:** Figma, Miro, paper prototypes, user interviews

## Syllabus

### Unit 1 — Why Design Thinking (5 lectures)
1. What design thinking is and where it comes from
2. Human-centred design — the core principle
3. The five stages — empathise, define, ideate, prototype, test
4. Divergent vs convergent thinking
5. Case studies — IDEO, Airbnb, and failure stories

### Unit 2 — Empathise and Define (5 lectures)
1. User research methods — interviews, observation, surveys
2. Building personas
3. Empathy maps
4. Problem framing — from symptom to need
5. Problem statements — point of view (POV) format

### Unit 3 — Ideate (5 lectures)
1. Brainstorming rules — quantity before quality
2. SCAMPER and other ideation techniques
3. Crazy 8s and sketching
4. Affinity mapping of ideas
5. Selecting ideas — impact vs effort

### Unit 4 — Prototype and Test (5 lectures)
1. Why prototype — learn fast, fail cheap
2. Low-fidelity prototyping — paper and sketches
3. Digital prototyping with Figma
4. Usability testing — tasks, think-aloud, notes
5. Iterating from feedback

### Unit 5 — Bringing It Together (5 lectures)
1. A complete design thinking sprint
2. Working with engineering teams
3. Communicating design decisions
4. Measuring success — usability metrics
5. Portfolio and case-study writing

## Practical Labs (15)

1. Form groups, choose a project domain
2. Conduct 3 user interviews
3. Build an empathy map
4. Create a persona
5. Write a POV problem statement
6. Brainstorm 30+ ideas in 10 minutes
7. Affinity-map the ideas into themes
8. Crazy 8s sketching session
9. Select the top 3 concepts
10. Paper prototype the top concept
11. Figma prototype — screen 1 and 2
12. Run a usability test with 3 users
13. Synthesise test findings
14. Iterate and improve the prototype
15. Final presentation and peer critique

## Assignments

1. Empathy report — interview summaries
2. Problem framing — persona and POV
3. Ideation board — 20+ ideas with selection rationale
4. Prototype and usability test report
5. Final case study — a documented design process
